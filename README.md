# SHEEP GO BONK

A vertical meme hyper-casual game starring one very angry sheep. Swipe to steer the dash, bonk every object on the lot, and build increasingly ridiculous chain reactions - box hits barrel, barrel hits sign, sign bounces back and bonks the sheep.

## Bonk everything

- Targets: cardboard boxes, barrels, road signs, crash dummies, giant watermelons, sofas - and whole buildings once the combo goes nuclear
- The BONK PROOF wall is the one thing that cannot be bonked: miss its gap and the run ends
- Combo milestones: 10 HEADBANG MODE, 20 SIGMA SHADES, 30 SPARK HORNS, 50 DISCO DASH - the horns keep growing forever
- Crazy events: GOLD RUSH, RUBBER MODE, BONK STORM, MEGA HORNS

## Five scenes, five palettes

FARM FIELD, SUNSET STRIP, DUST HIGHWAY, SUGAR PUSH and NEON VOID - each with its own color world, backdrop and ambient particles.

## Technical

- 100% single-file HTML5, zero dependencies, zero loaded files
- Every sound is synthesized live with Web Audio: comical bleats, per-object bonk sounds, an original 132 BPM loop that intensifies with your combo
- Squash and stretch, shockwaves, debris, camera shake and impact zoom
- Phone (swipe / drag) and desktop (mouse, A / D or arrow keys)
